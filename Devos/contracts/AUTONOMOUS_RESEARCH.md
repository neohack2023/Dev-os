# Autonomous Research Contract

Research is allowed when an active task exposes a blocker, capability gap, stale source, weak comparison, design dead-end, external uncertainty, or time-sensitive opportunity.

Research must be bounded by query/source budgets, prefer primary sources for factual claims, preserve URLs/dates/provenance, separate fact from inference and inspiration, and stop when more browsing is unlikely to change the disposition.

Research may produce evidence receipts, candidate opportunities, or an explicit no-op. It cannot directly promote canon, mutate product semantics, or bypass normal task/review governance.
